# 3. The Vigil

**Coverage:** third return through the morning after the rite  
**Recommended level:** unchanged  
**Permanent choice:** lantern keeper  
**Status:** desired campaign state built on the playable return loop

The third physical return changes lighting, tiles, NPC clothing, schedules and
available services without advance announcement.

> **Player commentary:** I came out expecting the usual town loop. Instead the
> lighting was amber, the forge cold, and name-cards hung from roofs that had
> been bare twenty minutes earlier. The surprise worked because I already knew
> the ordinary map.

## Changed-town checklist

| Location | Ordinary state | Vigil state | Missable detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bakery | Alicia working in apron | White-and-gold clothing | Hidden summon lantern |
| Forge | Active fire | Cold; wax warmed instead | Laura washed the soot away |
| Tankard | Tables indoors | Tables in street | Interior becomes unnaturally empty |
| Passage Office | Celina working | Registry closed | Her reaction to lost summons |
| Gate | Guard alone | Yukio present | Party-counting introduction |

## New character: Yukio

Yukio is a foil rather than an antagonist. They count the player's creatures,
test claims and resist the town's emotional explanations.

> **Player commentary:** I immediately disliked Yukio. They said, “You came
> back one voice short,” and sounded clinical about Saban. Ten minutes later
> they moved an unclaimed lantern out of the rain. The game did not tell me how
> to reconcile those details.

## Complete route

1. Revisit the changed versions of every available location.
2. Meet Yukio at the gate.
3. Enter the chapel and choose **Attend the Vigil**.
4. Follow the procession.
5. Hear the impossible third bell.
6. Inspect the unclaimed lantern after the rite.
7. Choose what to do with it.

The rite names living townspeople first and those remaining below second.
Something beneath St. Maria answers despite the tower containing only two
bells. The final lantern reads **THESTRA** in the protagonist's handwriting.

## Lantern outcomes

| Choice | Immediate result | Keeper | Later identity witness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light it | Thestra enters town remembrance | Chapel | Agnes |
| Hide it | Alicia may catch the player | Player / Alicia | Alicia |
| Give it to Yukio | Begins evidence-based trust | Yukio | Yukio |
| Leave it dark | Another person can claim it | Conditional | Conditional |

No choice resolves the mystery immediately. The keeper later becomes capable
of recognizing a false version of the player.

## Post-Vigil changes

- Wax remains on town stones.
- One lantern burns until morning.
- Alicia conceals a lantern bearing no human name.
- The Tankard insists only two bells sounded.
- Yukio independently confirms that the tower contains two bells.

## What this section must accomplish

- **Fantasy:** the hub has enough social reality to surprise the player with a
  private life and grief of its own.
- **Foil:** Yukio interprets the same evidence differently without merely
  opposing the player.
- **Complete-guide obligation:** every summon-specific lantern, service change,
  dialogue variant and lantern consequence must be listed.
- **Content debt:** festival tileset and lighting; complete NPC schedules;
  alternate indoor images; summon-specific lanterns; four-way ownership and
  the later false-player callback.
